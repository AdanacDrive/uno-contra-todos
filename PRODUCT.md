# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
Static HTML/CSS/JS (ES modules), no build step, published on GitHub Pages. Chosen with the user during brainstorming (see docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-24-web-juego-design.md).

## Users
Groups of 3 to 10 people aged 15 and up (friends, family) sitting around a table playing "UNO contra todos". A single phone sits in the middle of the table; whoever is closest taps it. They glance at it from about 1 m away in the middle of a noisy, physical game.

## Product Purpose
The digital component of a university creativity-course board game (Pontificia Universidad Javeriana, Creatividad e Innovación). The web plays the music, stops it several times per song at random, tells the table what to do at each stop ("Todos por el squishy", "Empieza el ponchado") and rolls the die for the Retroceder card. Success: the table never has to ask "who goes now?" and nobody can predict the stop.

## Positioning
The phone is the game master, not a music player: it decides the stops and the rule that follows each one.

## Operating Context
Physical game: number cards, a soft squishy ball, rounds of 3 songs, winner has fewest cards. Played in living rooms or classrooms, indoor light. Evaluated by the professor via a public link that must work on any phone.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- One phone, no accounts, no player names, no card counting.
- Bundled music: 12 reggaetón and cumbia tracks from Pixabay (Pixabay Content License); credits page lists each author.
- Optional: load your own songs from the phone (never uploaded).

## Brand Commitments
- Name: "UNO contra todos".
- Festive Colombian feel without using the Colombian flag literally (user decision). Pitch deck and infographic use cream, purple, yellow, blue and red.
- Spanish (Colombia), casual student voice, no hype.

## Product Principles
- Readable from a metre away in the middle of chaos: one instruction per screen.
- Never let the timing of a stop be predictable.
- The screen resolves arguments (who goes next, how many cards).
